We're running a drone job today. Before we talk through the aircraft, let's get the basics straight.

Job and roles: I'm the pilot in command. __________ is the visual observer. Everyone else stays clear of the takeoff, flight path and work area unless I say otherwise.

Stand-off distances: Non-crew stay at least 30 m back. Crew may come as close as 5 m from the drone. Closer than that only if I ask you to.

No fly-over: The drone does not fly over anyone. The landing zone must be clear before we approach to land.

Heads up: I'll call "heads up" on takeoff and landing. When you hear it, look up and keep clear.

Exclusion zone: No one under the drone, ever. Keep the landing zone guarded. If something looks wrong, step back and shout.

Load and rigging: I check the hook, ropes and slings before every lift. Hook-up and release crew wait for my call, keep hands clear of the hook, and step away once the load is hooked.

Swinging load: Never try to catch or steady a swinging load by hand. Step back and let me settle it.

Product: Today's product is on the label we've read. The label and SDS are the rules for this job: rate, PPE, buffer zones and re-entry. Applicable BC/AB pesticide rules apply too.

Drift: Spray must stay on target. No drift onto people, neighbouring properties, homes, water or other crops. If it's drifting, we stop.

Wind and inversion: We don't spray in gusty or rising wind, or in dead-calm conditions with a temperature inversion (cool still air near the ground, often early morning or evening). If in doubt, we wait.

Mixing and loading: Mix and load only at the mixing spot, upwind and away from water. Wear the PPE the label/SDS calls for when handling the product, mixing and filling the tank. Close containers right away.

Spills: If anything spills, stop, keep people away, contain it and clean it up per the label/SDS. Tell me right away.

Treated area: Nobody goes into the treated area during spraying or before the label re-entry time. Keep people and animals out.

Bags: Lift bags with your legs, not your back. Get help with heavy bags and keep the bag area tidy.

Dust: Filling the box makes dust. Wear a dust mask and eye protection when loading, and stand upwind.

Spinner: Stay clear of the spinning disc and the drone. Hands away from the spreader until the motors are off and I say it's safe.

Power lines and trees: We've checked for wires and tall trees. If you see a hazard I haven't called out, stop me.

Weather: Wind and gusts have to stay under our limit. If it picks up, we pause.

Battery: We land and swap at 30%. Don't wait for a low-battery warning.

Stop the mission: Anyone can stop the job. Contact a crew member, or wave your arms so we see you. If you need me to stop, say "STOP" loud and clear.

Emergency: If the aircraft goes down, nobody runs in until I say it's clear.
