Job and roles: I'm the pilot in command. Our visual observer is ____. Everyone else stays clear of the take-off and landing area unless I say otherwise.

Public safety: Keep the take-off and landing area clear; only crew go near it. Wherever we can, we mark the area with cones, tape or a spotter. If anyone from the public comes near, tell me right away and we'll hold until they're clear.

Heads up: I'll call "heads up" on take-off and landing. When you hear it, look up and keep clear of the props.

Weather: If it picks up, we land.

Battery: We plan to land with at least 30% battery left, or earlier if the app says to return.

Stop the flight: Anyone can stop the job. If you see a problem, a person, an aircraft or a bird coming in, say "STOP" loud and clear.

Emergency: If the drone goes down, nobody runs in until I say it's clear. If a battery smokes, stay back and upwind.

Any questions before we fly?
